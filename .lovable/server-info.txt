DOCUMENT PURPOSE AND FORMAT: This is the complete reference for the LowkeySMP project, written for AI assistants so they can continue the work without asking the owner to repeat himself. It describes the Minecraft event, every plugin feature, the admin tools, the website, the plugin to website link, the history of changed decisions, the current open items and the owner's working preferences. Status date: 2 November 2026. The event starts 7 November 2026, so about 5 days remain. Each paragraph is one block of text on one line, no blank lines. Where a value is called default or approx it can be changed in game or in code, verify in the code before relying on it.
PROJECT IN ONE PARAGRAPH: LowkeySMP (tagline Lowkey Peak, project folder name VriendenSMP) is a Dutch hardcore style survival event for the owner's friends, inspired by CreatorSMP seasons 3 and 4. Players are split into two teams, Noord (red) and Zuid (blue), on one big island split by an ice wall. After a grace period every death is an elimination. Eliminated players get a second chance on a separate Verliezerseiland world, and the event ends with a Hoofdfinale in a separate finale world with a shrinking world border, last player standing wins. The owner is Gaspard (Dutch speaking, Belgium). The owner Minecraft account is Lowkey_SMP (replaced R3ktify, who was the first CREW member). Server host is WiseHosting, Paper server, plugin api-version 26.1 (Paper 26.x), about 2 GB RAM at the time of the first tests, to be upgraded.
DATES AND TIMELINE (Seizoen 1): 7 nov Server open (day one starts at 14:00, the owner presses launch himself, no automatic launch). 11 nov Verliezerseiland start. 13 nov IJsmuur smelt (the ice wall simply disappears that day, borders dropped). 14 nov Nether open. 15 nov End open. 18 nov Finale Verliezerseiland. 21 nov Hoofdfinale. Event period 7 to 21 november 2026. The earlier plan of 30 october to 7 november was replaced. The lobby has a timeline hologram showing one card per event, browsable per player with arrows, date only, with spinning block icons (door, skull, blue ice, obsidian, end portal frame, totem for the losers finale, gold block for the main finale). The owner wants the resource pack icon version of that hologram deleted later but not yet.
WORLD CONCEPT: One big island with many biomes and structures, vanilla world border around it, split by an ice wall (like CreatorSMP season 3). The ice wall is bumpy, follows the terrain and runs through caves, so wall phases (4 melt steps) were dropped, it just disappears on 13 nov. WorldGuard region teamgrens protects 10 blocks around the wall (no building or breaking, flags block-break, block-place, entry, enderpearl, chorus-fruit-teleport). The border checklist before dropping the borders is: remove those WorldGuard flags, then press Grenzen droppen. There is no mountain anymore. Phantoms were considered and dropped with the old loser island design.
TEAMS: Noord is red, Zuid is blue. Noord icon is a red square with a grey crown, Zuid icon is a teal blue square with a white cream cross (exact images supplied by the owner, crisp, same size as other icons). Team spawns are set with /lowkey setspawn noord or zuid. Team chat is separate: only Noord sees Noord chat and only Zuid sees Zuid chat, except in the lobby and except after the borders are dropped (then chat is global). Chat names are red for Noord and blue for Zuid. Until the borders drop, private messages (/msg and aliases, /r) between Noord and Zuid are blocked with a LOWKEY badge message. Players without a team who press the lobby item get the message that they are not in a team yet. Respawn without bed or anchor (grace deaths) goes to the own team spawn.
GRACE PERIOD: Every player has a personal grace window of 60 minutes of playtime (config grace-minutes, default 60) starting at their own first join (not global). During grace a death is a normal respawn, with a public message LOWKEY naam is doodgegaan, and the player gets a start message with the LOWKEY badge (Je kunt voor de komende 60 minuten respawnen). A grace badge (light blue or white square with a gold frame, wide icon) marks players still in grace in tab and nametag but never in chat, and it is hidden in the lobby. When a player own grace runs out only that player gets Je kunt niet meer respawnen. Grace can be set per player (/lowkey grace speler minuten), for everybody (/lowkey graceall [minuten], also a button in Teams and in the check menu), and offline players are reset through a version counter when they join. Deaths in grace are NOT reported to the website death queue. ISLAND GRACE (since 0.46.0): the first time a player enters the Verliezerseiland he gets his own grace there, default 120 minutes (config losers-grace-minutes), counted only while he is online outside the lobby, shown with the same grace badge. A death in that time is a normal respawn on the island spawn (global message LOWKEY naam is doodgegaan, not reported to the website). The island grace stops for everybody in the island world when the island finale starts. Reviving a player also resets the island grace flag so tests can be repeated.
ELIMINATION: After grace a death is an elimination (config eliminate-on-death true). There is no respawn screen: death is cancelled, the player is kept alive at full health, items and xp drop, blood particles burst, a big red UITGESCHAKELD title (the owner own artwork, red block letters with black outline) is shown, the public chat message is the red LOWKEY badge plus naam plus the suffix is uitgeschakeld! (config eliminated-suffix), and the dead player gets a private message that they can spectate. Spectator time in the main world is 60 seconds (adjustable in the menu), after that the player is sent to the lobby for good and can never walk back into the main world. If there is no lobby the player is kicked. Dead players stay listed in grey italic in the team menu and are sorted last in the tab list. A command and menu button can fully revive somebody (/lowkey revive speler), mainly for testing. Deaths in the finale world have no timer: the player spectates until the finale and winner show end and then goes to the lobby together with everybody. Since 0.46.0 every real elimination (main world, island, finale) plays a low wither spawn sound for everybody online in every world, and the red elimination message is shown in every world (main world, Verliezerseiland, lobby, finale world). Spectate rules after an elimination: if the finale of the world where you died is running (main finale in the finale world, island finale on the island) you keep watching until the finale ends and then go to the lobby together with everybody. If no finale is running in that world you get the normal 60 seconds (spectateSeconds) and then the lobby, also on the Verliezerseiland. A finale only counts for the world where you die.
LOBBY: A separate void world made with /lowkey lobby create. Every join sends the player to the lobby. Lobby protection: no breaking or placing, no dropping or picking up, no PvP, no snow or freezing, no mob changes, full HP always, infinite saturation, players walk through each other (collision off in the lobby, on elsewhere), the mace is disabled and must never be in a lobby inventory. Hotbar items: the LowkeySMP nether star in slot 5 (cannot be moved or dropped, trying to drop shows LOWKEY je mag geen items droppen hier). Right click opens a dialog Wil je naar het main eiland teleporteren with Ja and Nee. Before launch it shows the red message de server is nog niet open and no dialog. After launch Ja teleports the player to their last known position in the game (a first time player to their team spawn). A signed rulebook (real Book and Quill style, written by the plugin) sits in slot 9, forced read on first join, tab Regels in the menu, /lowkey rulesreset makes everybody read it again. Eliminated players in the lobby get a red Verliezerseiland nether star (see Verliezerseiland) and their LowkeySMP star tells them they cannot join because they are dead. A lobby has its own tab list, chat and join and leave messages, separate from the main world, the Verliezerseiland and the finale world (each world group only sees itself, staff and crew see everything).
LAUNCH AND SWITCHES: /lowkey launch (also in the menu, asks confirmation, /lowkey launch reset resets it) teleports everybody with a team to their team spawn with the title Veel succes! and the title Welkom bij: followed by the LowkeySMP logo. /lowkey server close or open shuts the whole server for non crew while the owner edits (only crew and the owner can join). /lowkey mainworld close or open closes only the main world, everybody goes to the lobby, reopening does not teleport anybody (players return themselves with the star). /lowkey nether open or close shows the red title De Nether is geopend and the red chat message. /lowkey end open or close does the same for the End. /lowkey border open or close drops or restores the borders (ice wall). Server hours: the server is open daily from 09:00 to 00:00, non crew joining outside hours are denied at login with exactly De server is open van 09:00 tot 00:00. Kom later terug, players still online at 00:00 are kicked, closing warnings with the LOWKEY badge at 10, 3 and 1 minute before the server sluit (the verb sluit is deliberate). Crew can always join.
MACE (only one on the server): Vanilla recipe, no custom recipe. When the first mace is crafted every other way to get one is blocked. A title (not chat) shows Noord heeft de mace ontgrendeld in red or Zuid heeft de mace ontgrendeld in blue for about 3 seconds with a wither spawn sound to everyone online, a crafter without team gives De mace is gecraft. If the mace is destroyed (burned, despawned, lava, cactus, void) it is gone for good and a new one cannot be crafted. It can be dropped and moved between inventories, all vanilla enchants allowed. Cooldown is 10 minutes shown as the item cooldown overlay, it starts only after a real smash attack (vanilla threshold 1.5 blocks fall) that hits a player (teammates count, friendly fire is on), it keeps running when the mace changes owner, it only runs in the main world, and it is disabled in the lobby. State persists over restarts. Menu tab Mace with the current holder, on and off, cooldown set and reset, give, reset and a test button. Commands /lowkey mace status|aan|uit|cooldown|cooldownreset|give|reset|test. The mace is an object of the class MaceManager inside LowkeyCore.java.
TOTEMS: Max 2 Totems of Undying per player server wide (config max-totems), checked in inventory, crafting slots, containers and shulker contents, with an actionbar message. Since version 0.42.0 totems can not be put into shulker boxes at all (click, shift click, drag, hotbar swap and hoppers are blocked, message Je mag geen totems in shulker boxes steken, totems already inside a carried shulker are dropped out).
VERLIEZERSEILAND: A separate normal generated survival world (not void) for players who died on the main world, so they get another chance. It has its own tab list, chat, join and leave messages, permanently bad scary weather, and phantoms are not used. Create with /lowkey losers create [seed] (an existing island is replaced), tp, setspawn, open, close, return, returnall, delete, all also in the Verliezerseiland menu. Everybody eliminated gets a red nether star called Verliezerseiland in the lobby; while the island is closed the star says it is not open yet. Dying on the island is final (spectator, message definitief uitgeschakeld, flagged losersOut, never returns). The owner decides himself when too many died: buttons to bring back everybody still alive on the island or to pick individual players, and the number of winners is adjustable. At the island finale (18 nov) the adjustable number of winners is sent back to the main world to play the Hoofdfinale and then the star is removed. The island finale has its own winner show (test with 3 test names).
HOOFDFINALE (21 nov): It does not run in the main world (the border there is square, the map is rectangular and the centre was on Noord). It runs in a separate finale world named lowkey_finale, same generator, seed -1269089582167819796 (config finale-seed, the seed of an existing world cannot be changed, delete and recreate the world if it is wrong, /lowkey check turns red when it does not match). The owner builds the 4 walls around the Noord area himself, sets the vanilla border and the middle point, the world stays after the finale. Everybody online in the main world and not eliminated goes to the one middle point with their own main inventory, lobby, offline and Verliezerseiland players do not take part, leaving during the finale means out. First 5 minutes (adjustable) no PvP, no mace, border does not shrink, fall damage lava and mobs still work, chat warns PvP is off (zoek je teamgenoten en maak je inventory klaar) and warns at 3 minutes, 1 minute and 10 seconds. Then the vanilla border shrinks from the start size to a target centre and size (final about 30 blocks across, adjustable with a button that uses the owner own position as target), continuous shrinking first then rounds in the last 3 minutes of shrink, pause 30 seconds with a blue border, shrink again with a red border, chat De border is aan het krimpen. Start shows a title and ONE chat block with a single LOWKEY badge (headline, border line, players left, PvP line for the finale world). Remaining player announcements at 10, 5, 3 and 2 players. When one player is left a title naam heeft LowkeySMP gewonnen with fireworks around the winner, a test button exists, ties are resolved by the last deaths. About 21 seconds after the winner show everybody in the finale world goes to the lobby together. Commands /lowkey finale menu|start|pauze|hervat|stop|status|test. Who is sent to the finale world at the start: only players who are online in the main world group (main, nether, end), not eliminated, not out on the island, and in survival or adventure mode. Players in the lobby, in creative or spectator (admins) and eliminated players are NOT teleported. The start is refused with a red message when the border of the world is above 100000 blocks (the vanilla default of about 60 million means it was never set), set it first with /worldborder set 2000 in that world. /lowkey check shows the finale border size. The border shrink sizes are clamped between 1 block and the start size and are logged in the console.
SPECTATING THE FINALES (since 0.44.0, extended in 0.45.0): Players who are already out can type /spectate (aliases /spec and /kijken) while a finale is running or paused. It works for the main Hoofdfinale (to the finale world middle) and for the Verliezerseiland finale (to the island spawn). Allowed are players flagged losersOut and eliminated players who are not playing on the island, not players still alive on the island or in the main game. The viewer is put in spectator mode, the return place and game mode are remembered, the message says Gebruik /spectate leave om te stoppen met kijken, /spectate leave returns, and viewers also go back when the finale ends (about 21 seconds after the winner show) or when the finale is stopped. Viewers are hidden from the tab list of every normal player (staff still see them) so finalists can see who is still alive, and they never count as alive. At finale start every eliminated player gets a chat hint about /spectate. KIJK-OOG hologram: a big spinning Eye of Ender with the text Wil je graag de finale spectaten? and Klik om mee te kijken in the lobby. It does not exist at all (completely invisible) until the admin places it and it switches itself on when any finale starts and off again when the finale ends (since 0.45.1, it can still be toggled by hand), commands /lowkey kijkoog plaats|aan|uit|weg and two buttons in the Finale menu (Kijk-oog plaatsen, Kijk-oog aanzetten or uitzetten). Clicking it (left or right click) runs /spectate for that player. Finale start messages (since 0.45.1): only the players who are in the finale world get the title, sound and the chat block. Everybody else gets one short LOWKEY line, for players who are out: De finale is begonnen! Druk op het grote oog of doe /spectate om mee te kijken, for the rest: De finale is begonnen!
TEAM EVENT (team division): /lowkey event start or the menu button Team event. Everybody online at that moment (lobby included) takes part and gets a new team, players who leave stay out and are fixed by hand. Everybody camera is locked on one fixed position (spectator camera on an invisible stand), the Noord logo is shown big on the left and the Zuid logo on the right with titles, a shuffle animation through the skins and names with sounds slows down until one is picked, the name turns red or blue and the head and name are placed under the logo, repeated until everybody has a team, then teams apply automatically. Takes about 2 to 3 minutes for 20 players. Head layout adapts (more columns and smaller heads) so heads and names never overlap, sneaking and leaving the camera are blocked during the event, test mode with fake names, /lowkey event test|stop|camera|teams|reset, the menu shows the split with all names and has a reset teams button. AUTO TEAM (since 0.46.0): a setting that gives a team to every player who joins without one (not crew, not eliminated). The player goes to the smaller team and to a random team when both are equal. At joining the titles Jij bent: and then NOORD (red) or ZUID (blue) are shown with a sound and a chat line. It switches itself on when a real team event reaches its outro (not in test mode) and can be toggled with /lowkey autoteam aan|uit|status and with the button Auto team in the Teams menu. /lowkey check shows its state. It is stored in state.yml as auto-team.
CHAT, BADGES AND TABLIST: LOWKEY badges are pink and purple like the logo, except the death message badge and the Nether messages which stay red (an admin test switch exists, /lowkey badge huidig|rood|roze). All icons are sharp cornered resource pack glyphs taken from real Minecraft assets or supplied by the owner, never self drawn. Badge order next to a name is always (CREW) (GRACE) (TEAM). Tab list always shows every badge a player has, chat shows only the team badge, except a crew member without team who still shows CREW in chat. CREW is a wide purple badge, crew names are purple in tab and chat, crew moves silently between worlds. Tab list order is crew, grace Noord, grace Zuid, grace without team, Noord, Zuid, no team, dead last, alphabetical inside each group. Tab header is the LowkeySMP logo, footer has Noord and Zuid counters (config tab-team-width 120), online count, the website line, no Nether or border lines. Lobby players only see lobby players in tab, main world players only main world. Join and leave messages are green plus and red minus icon lines per world group. The MOTD updates automatically with online status and player count (slightly left of centre, accepted). Donation broadcaster: about 10 varied white messages in random order every 15 minutes with the LOWKEY badge, core idea send Gaspard a private message to donate, every cent helps, only a few mention privébericht, /lowkey donate sends one for testing. Website line in the tab and a periodic website message (config website-url and website-interval-minutes). /lowkey say bericht sends a custom message to everybody with the LOWKEY badge.
ANONYMOUS ADMIN MESSAGES (since 0.45.0): Normal player whispers (/msg, /tell, /w, /whisper, /pm, /m, /dm) are NOT touched by the plugin and keep the vanilla look. Existing rules still apply to them: no whispers between the lobby, the main world and other worlds for normal players, no whispers between Noord and Zuid before the borders drop, crew and admins see everything and every whisper as a gray [spy] line, whispers are logged to the console. New: an admin can write to a player as if the server itself speaks, with /lowkey tell naam bericht (also the Berichten menu button Anoniem bericht with a name and a message field). The player sees the LOWKEY badge line LowkeySMP naar Jij with a soft chime and a first time hint to answer with /r bericht. The answer goes only to the admin who wrote (shown as Naam naar LowkeySMP), or to every admin online if that admin left or it was the console. A normal whisper ends the LowkeySMP conversation for /r. Without a LowkeySMP conversation /r answers the last normal whisper partner through /msg.
HOLOGRAMS: The big LowkeySMP countdown hologram (logo with countdown to 7 november 14:00 below it) is perfect and must not change, commands /lowkey countdown spawn|remove|set|size, no countdown in the MOTD. The playtime leaderboard of the main world (title Leaderbord van speeltijd, rows like #1 - Naam - dag:uur:minuut, no seconds, crew skipped by default, resettable) stays upright and only turns horizontally, commands /lowkey leaderboard spawn|remove|refresh|size|reset. The timeline hologram is described under dates. The Kijk-oog hologram for spectating the finales is described under SPECTATING THE FINALES.
ADMIN ACCESS AND MENU: Only operators with permission lowkey.admin use /lowkey. Typing /lowkey opens the admin panel (dialog menu with buttons and real vanilla item icons). The owner account always has access, other admins use /lowkey login and a pin (/lowkey pin pincode, the owner defines the pin, never store it in documents). Crew are listed in config crew, server wide closed access in config closed-access, owners in login.owners. Menu categories: Server, Berichten (countdown, eigen bericht, donatie, website, leaderboard, badge test), Mace, Teams (Noord left, Zuid right, per player actions, move with confirm, grace for everyone, offline players wipe), Wereld (lobby teleport, spawns, Nether, End, borders, Verliezerseiland), Finale, Event, Regels, Check, Status, Testen en herstellen (revive, return from island, reset teams, close main world, launch, reset switches). Standing rule from the owner: every new feature or command must also get a menu button.
NEW ADMIN TOOLS (0.42.0 to 0.44.0): /lowkey status shows TPS, MSPT, memory, uptime, players per world. /lowkey check (menu Check, or /lowkey check chat) lists green yellow red items before the start: TPS, memory, lobby, Noord and Zuid spawn, seed, finale world seed and middle, server and main world open, launch state, Nether and End closed, borders, WorldGuard, mace, rulebook, Verliezerseiland, finale and team event state, teams, offline players, dead players, grace, website link. The check menu has buttons for grace for everyone, everyone reads the rules again, reset spawnpoints (/lowkey spawnreset wipes saved last positions and online bed spawns), reset stats and a Teams shortcut. The Teams menu has Offline wissen which deletes the plugin data of every offline non staff player (used to remove test bots). /lowkey verban speler main|verliezerseiland|beide lobby|volledig bans somebody from worlds, with the question whether he may stay in the lobby, unban with /lowkey verbanopheffen, /lowkey tolobby speler kicks someone to the lobby, /lowkey give speler gives the lobby star, /lowkey seed seed|random|cancel handles the main world seed.

Always give complete files, never diffs. Plugin data lives in plugins/LowkeyCore: config.yml, state, menu.yml, players/uuid.yml (team, grace, eliminated, stashed inventory, last location), site-queue.json (messages for the website that failed), stats-baseline.yml (stat reset baseline). RESOURCE PACK: Pack named lowkey-pack, hosted on GitHub because there is no other host, loaded with a sha1 (use the newest sha1 after every change, an old sha1 makes the pack fail). It holds all badge and icon glyphs in the private use range (LOWKEY badge E001, red badge E009, team logos E060 and E061, menu icons E040 to E053 and E070 to E080, mace E053), the logo and title art (UITGESCHAKELD, Welkom bij, LowkeySMP), custom hearts (FAKE_HARDCORE_HEART) and sharp cornered icons. BEHEER (admin of the site): The Beheer button in the footer opens a login page that only asks for a password (Welkom terug), the session persists, there is a logout. Behind it: Overzicht (visitors today, 7 days, total, chart of 14 days, players alive and dead, latest deaths, waiting whitelist requests), Deaths goedkeuren, Whitelist (approving adds the player to the player list), Spelers en deaths, Aankondigingen (add, edit, pin, delete, several photos), Video's (upload, choose Main trailer or Uitlegvideo), Donaties, Openingsuren, Skin zoeken, Instellingen (server IP and the opening time that drives the countdown). An edit mode for admins lets every text be clicked and changed, blocks on the home page be moved, hidden or added, and the timeline events edited, saved in the table site_content as key and json value. The owner never wants the old technical admin address mentioned, always say beheer.
PLUGIN TO WEBSITE LINK: The plugin POSTs json to https://lowkeysmp.lovable.app/api/plugin with header Authorization Bearer token, the same secret is stored as PLUGIN_TOKEN on the site and as site.token in config.yml. Types: ping returns ok true. death sends player, uuid, eventId, diedAt (ISO), cause (the English Minecraft death message such as Alex fell from a high place) and location main or island, the site inserts a pending row in death_reports, the owner approves in Deaths goedkeuren (can edit time, cause and place), then the player is marked dead, shows in the death log and on the timeline, and a push message goes out. Only real eliminations are reported (not grace deaths), main world and finale deaths as main, Verliezerseiland deaths as island. stats sends players with uuid, name, playtimeSeconds, kills, totemPops, jumps and kmWalked (walk, sprint and sneak distance) every 5 minutes, stored in the table player_stats, shown on the Statistieken page as a searchable list sortable by playtime, totem pops, kills, jumps and kilometres (no deaths column, no death causes). statsReset (since 0.44.0) sends everybody at zero and the site must delete all rows of player_stats first and then insert the sent rows. Failed messages are queued in site-queue.json and retried every minute. Commands: /lowkey site test, stats, queue, reset confirm. Lowkey_SMP and anything matching site.stats-exclude never appears in stats.
STATS RESET: Minecraft statistics themselves are never changed. /lowkey site reset confirm (also the Check menu button Stats resetten) stores the current numbers of every known player as a baseline in stats-baseline.yml, later stats are sent as current minus baseline, and sends statsReset to the site. Do this right before launch so playtime from before 7 november is gone. Test bots that are still in the plugin player list are synced too, so wipe offline players first in the Teams menu, and the website side also gets a way to delete players.
WHY ONLY ONE DEATH ARRIVED ON THE SITE: The owner died twice quickly (once on the main world and once on the Verliezerseiland) and got one report. The plugin sends both. The cause was the rule given to the website that a pending report of the same player in the last 2 minutes is skipped. The correct rule is to skip only when the same player, same location and same diedAt (or the same eventId) already exist.
TESTING HISTORY: Test bots from the FakePlayerPlugin were tried (v0.27.0), became a mess and were removed from LowkeyCore in v0.28.0, but about 100 bot players were used again later for load tests, which left bot names in the team list and the stats. Remove them with the Teams menu Offline wissen, the stats reset and the website delete tool.
